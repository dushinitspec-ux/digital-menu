## Q0. Per-competitor fact sheets: what does each of the 6 platforms offer (pricing, devices, menu features, reviews, EU presence, payments)?

### Takeaway
All 6 brands still sell in 2026 except **Raydiant**: **Displai Systems** bought its assets (products, technology, customer contracts) in May 2025, and raydiant.com now redirects to displai.ai. Published per-screen prices run from about $6 (Look, high-volume yearly) through $8–16 (Yodeck), $9–45 (OptiSigns), $14–25 (NoviSign, Sept 2026) and $20–36 (ScreenCloud), up to about $49–59 (Displai, quote-only). Every price I found is in USD; no EUR price list was confirmed for any vendor.

## Q1. What is the realistic per-screen monthly price range among these leaders in 2026?

### Takeaway
List prices run from **about $6 to about $59 per screen per month**. For a small café with 1–3 screens, the realistic software price is **$8–15** (Yodeck, OptiSigns, Look, NoviSign entry tiers), **$20–36** for ScreenCloud, and **$49–59+** for quote-only Displai (ex-Raydiant). Menu-relevant POS sync usually sits in the higher tiers, at about $13.50–17.

### Cited Findings
- Yodeck: free for 1 screen; then $8 / $12 / $16 (Basic / Premium / Enterprise); Enterprise+ custom; +$1 on Premium and Enterprise from 1 Apr 2026 — [Yodeck docs](https://www.yodeck.com/docs/user-manual/how-is-yodeck-priced/); [Brix Signage](https://brixsignage.com/blog/yodeck-price-increase-2026/).

## Q3. Where are they weak for a small café owner?

### Takeaway
The main weaknesses for a small café are:
- setup and navigation complexity (Yodeck, OptiSigns, NoviSign);
- price creep, with POS sync locked into higher tiers (OptiSigns Pro Plus) or expensive plans (ScreenCloud $20–36; Displai $49+, quote-only, annual contracts);
- dependence on the internet, leading to outages (ScreenCloud);
- limited templates and customization (Look, NoviSign; ScreenCloud's basic design tool);
- USD-only, US-centric POS coverage, with no confirmed EUR pricing or EU payment methods.

### Cited Findings
- Yodeck: users find initial setup and navigation complex; app functionality for managing presentations is lacking — [G2 Yodeck pros/cons](https://www.g2.com/products/yodeck/reviews?qs=pros-and-cons). No free tier — [digitalsignage.com](https://digitalsignage.com/digital_signage/docs/guides/screencloud-pricing-2026/).
- OptiSigns: "hard to set up, even for tech-savvy users"; data-integration issues (Power BI) — [Capterra OptiSigns](https://www.capterra.com/p/173450/OptiSigns/reviews/). POS integration starts at Pro Plus and is set up through an API gateway with tokens — [digitalsignage.com](https://digitalsignage.com/digital_signage/docs/guides/best-menu-board-software-2026/).

### Inferences
- These openings for a new café-focused product are inferred from the complaints above, not tested:
  - pair a TV in about 2 minutes with no extra box (consumer Tizen, webOS, Google TV, Fire TV);
  - a **price-list-first editor** (edit a table, and the layout updates), so no Sheets or Zapier is needed;
  - **premium motion and video templates** made for cafés, bars and hookah lounges;
  - **guaranteed offline playback**;
  - **EUR pricing with SEPA, PayPal and EU-local payment methods**;
  - EU-relevant POS (SumUp, Lightspeed, local POS systems) even in the low tier.
- The incumbents' free tiers (Yodeck: 1 screen; OptiSigns: 1–3 screens) set the bar: a café owner can start for €0. A new product likely needs a comparable free or trial entry point.
